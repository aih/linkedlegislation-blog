Legislation that will facilitate the lawmaking process itself. In this category are bills that have been prepared by non-partisan expert agencies in order to advance the work of Congress. For example, the Law Revision Counsel has prepared “clean-up” bills that simply correct the citations in various laws and U.S. Code sections to point to the correct authority. These are the equivalent of “bug fixes” to correct errors that plague U.S. law and make it more difficult to automate legal analysis.
